WoR Mod Manager 1.0.7

- Fix for "Couldn't establish context: SendOutfitterUserData failed" when joining a server: a saved uniform made under another mod (or an older version of one) can name items the mods you have enabled now don't have, and War of Rights then refuses to join any server that asks for that uniform. The manager now checks your saved uniforms when it starts, after you change mods, and when you press Play. Any it finds are set aside (that slot gets a fresh kit) and you're told which. They come back by themselves when you enable the mod that has those items again, unless you've saved a new kit for that uniform in the meantime. Your other uniforms aren't touched.
