WoR Mod Manager 1.0.3

- Do several things at once: install or update more than one mod at the same time, and keep switching mods on and off, adding, removing and uninstalling others while downloads run.
- Each mod shows its own progress and Cancel, the same on its Red's Corner and My Library cards. A mod you start installing appears in My Library right away.
- The status bar lists everything in progress.
