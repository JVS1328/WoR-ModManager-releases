WoR Mod Manager 1.2.0

- The uniform customizer now shows your soldier in 3D beside the item lists. Pick a uniform and he's wearing it; change any item and he changes straight away. Drag to turn him, use the wheel to zoom in towards the head, double-click to reset.
- He's built from the game's own files the way the game loads them, so the mods you have enabled are what you see: their uniforms, items, models and textures. He stands in the game's own third-person idle pose, with its cloth, leather and metal (brass buckles and buttons, steel and tin), lit like a photo studio.
- Selecting "Head" shows your head on its own, close up, with new controls for it: the face (every head the game and your enabled mods have) and sliders for age, hair colour, eye colour, skin tone and dirt. They're saved into the uniform profile when you press Save, the same settings the game's own customization screen writes. Anything you don't change, and the rest of the file, is kept exactly as it was, so profiles from earlier versions carry on working.
- Age blends the game's own young, middle-aged and old faces, and dirt uses the game's own dirt mask. Hair colour follows the game's hair colours; skin tone and eye colour are close approximations, because the game doesn't ship those shaders in a form the manager can read.
- The 3D preview uses WebView2, the Edge engine built into Windows 10 and 11. Without it the customizer works exactly as before, and the panel tells you why there's no preview.
